# Week 5 · Urban analytics

**Big question:** can AI measure how a city looks and feels?

**Slides:** in preparation · **Pre-reading:** [Week 5 readings](../../readings/week05-urban-analytics.md) · **Lab:** [Street-level greenery and deprivation in Glasgow](../../labs/week05-urban-analytics/)

## Learning outcomes

1. Explain how street-level imagery, satellite imagery and mobility data are combined in urban analytics.
2. Compute a Green View Index from street-level images with semantic segmentation.
3. Link image-derived indicators to Scottish Index of Multiple Deprivation data zones and test for spatial patterns.

## Lecture highlights

- **Hook:** a pairwise vote: which of two Glasgow streets looks safer? Could a model learn your answer?
- **Concept bursts:** sensing the city from images (perception, greenery, building age); graph neural networks for traffic; combining image, mobility and census data.
- **Signature activity, "proxy court":** groups put a proxy on trial, for example "cars visible in street views predict how a neighbourhood votes" (Gebru et al., 2017). Prosecution and defence argue its validity, then the class rules.
- **Ethics minute:** the ecological fallacy, and the risk of stigmatising neighbourhoods with automated scores.
