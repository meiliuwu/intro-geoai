# Week 4 · Big geodata mining

**Big question:** what can digital traces tell us about places and people?

**Slides:** in preparation · **Pre-reading:** [Week 4 readings](../../readings/week04-big-geodata-mining.md) · **Lab:** [Mining Glasgow's digital traces](../../labs/week04-big-geodata-mining/)

## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the main sources of big geodata (volunteered geographic information, social sensing, mobility traces) and their biases.
2. Extract and cluster OpenStreetMap points of interest with density-based clustering.
3. Learn place embeddings from co-occurring points of interest, and mine stops and trips from GPS trajectories.

## Lecture highlights

- **Hook:** poll: how many location points would someone need to identify you? The answer is revealed in Week 7.
- **Concept bursts:** data sources and their sampling biases; density clustering (DBSCAN, HDBSCAN); place embeddings (Place2Vec); trajectory mining (stops, origin–destination flows).
- **Signature activity, "data detectives":** each group gets one data-source card (OpenStreetMap, fitness-app heatmap, geotagged posts, mobile-phone records, bike-share trips) and maps who is over- and under-represented, followed by a gallery walk.
- **Ethics minute:** scraping, terms of service and consent.
