# Week 7 · Ethics of geospatial AI

**Big question:** who benefits, who is exposed, and who is missing?

**Slides:** in preparation · **Pre-reading:** [Week 7 readings](../../readings/week07-ethics.md) · **Lab:** [Auditing GeoAI](../../labs/week07-ethics/)

## Learning outcomes

1. Identify privacy, bias, transparency, energy and reproducibility risks across the GeoAI lifecycle.
2. Measure re-identification risk in trajectory data, and the privacy–utility trade-off of geomasking.
3. Write a model card for a GeoAI model built earlier in the course.

## Lecture highlights

- **Hook:** the answer to the Week 4 poll. In de Montjoye et al.'s data, four spatio-temporal points were enough to single out 95% of people.
- **Concept bursts:** risks at each stage of the lifecycle: data (consent, representativeness), model (bias, explainability), deployment (surveillance, dual use) and environment (energy and carbon).
- **Signature activity, stakeholder role-play:** "A city council wants to combine street-view AI and mobile-phone data to prioritise regeneration funding." Groups play the council, a residents' association, a technology vendor, a data-protection officer and an academic adviser, and must agree conditions for approval or rejection.
- **Wrap:** course reflection. Each student writes one principle they will apply in their project and dissertation.
