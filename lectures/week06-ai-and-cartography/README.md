# Week 6 · AI and cartography

**Big question:** who is the cartographer when AI draws the map?

**Slides:** in preparation · **Pre-reading:** [Week 6 readings](../../readings/week06-ai-and-cartography.md) · **Lab:** [Generative maps](../../labs/week06-ai-and-cartography/)

## Learning outcomes

1. Describe where AI enters the map-making pipeline: generalisation, style transfer, labelling and generation.
2. Run an image-to-image GAN that turns satellite images into map tiles, and evaluate its output.
3. Critique AI-generated maps against cartographic principles, and recognise deepfake geography.

## Lecture highlights

- **Hook, "real or fake?":** six map tiles and satellite images, some generated. Students vote on each before the reveal.
- **Concept bursts:** AI across the cartographic pipeline (generalisation, symbolisation, label placement, reading historical maps); GANs and diffusion models for maps; AI agents that write mapping code.
- **Signature activity, map critique:** groups critique AI-made maps with a cartographic checklist (projection, classification, colour, legend, honesty about uncertainty), then redesign one.
- **Ethics minute:** provenance and watermarking. Should AI-generated maps be labelled?
