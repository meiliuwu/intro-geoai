## How to read for this course

1. **Skim, then focus.** Read the abstract, figures and conclusion first, then the sections named in the list.
2. **Annotate.** Use the annotation prompts in each list. Your notes and questions in the class's shared annotation space shape the lecture's opening.
3. **Bring one question** you would put to the authors.
4. **Reading leads.** From Week 2, one group of four leads the paper clinic: they set the questions for the expert groups and chair the report-back.

## Getting access

The papers are linked by DOI, not copied here, because most are under publisher copyright. Links marked **Open access** are free to read. For the others, sign in through your university library (for University of Glasgow students, via the Library's search or the publisher's institutional login).
