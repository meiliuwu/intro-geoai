# Week 1 · Model architectures for GeoAI

**Big question:** what makes an AI model *spatial*?

## Core readings

1. Janowicz, K., Gao, S., McKenzie, G., Hu, Y. and Bhaduri, B. (2020) GeoAI: spatially explicit artificial intelligence techniques for geographic knowledge discovery and beyond. *International Journal of Geographical Information Science* 34(4), 625–636. https://doi.org/10.1080/13658816.2019.1684500
   *Why:* defines GeoAI and what makes a method spatially explicit. Read in full (it is short).
2. LeCun, Y., Bengio, Y. and Hinton, G. (2015) Deep learning. *Nature* 521, 436–444. https://doi.org/10.1038/nature14539
   *Why:* the classic overview of convolutional and recurrent networks. Focus on the sections on convolutional and recurrent neural networks.
3. Mai, G., Janowicz, K., Hu, Y., Gao, S., Yan, B., Zhu, R., Cai, L. and Lao, N. (2022) A review of location encoding for GeoAI: methods and applications. *International Journal of Geographical Information Science* 36(4), 639–673. https://doi.org/10.1080/13658816.2021.2004602
   *Why:* how a neural network can "read" a coordinate. Read sections 1–3.

## Annotation prompts

- Which of the tests of spatial explicitness in Janowicz et al. would a plain CNN classifying satellite tiles pass?
- Why is feeding raw latitude and longitude into a neural network not enough?
- Smith asked in 1984 how AI could help solve geographical problems. Which of his questions are still open?
