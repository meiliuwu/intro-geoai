# Week 2 · Remote sensing and Earth observation

**Big question:** can a model see land use from space, and where does it fail?

## Core readings

1. Zhu, X.X., Tuia, D., Mou, L., Xia, G.-S., Zhang, L., Xu, F. and Fraundorfer, F. (2017) Deep learning in remote sensing: a comprehensive review and list of resources. *IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Magazine* 5(4), 8–36. https://doi.org/10.1109/MGRS.2017.2762307
   *Why:* the standard map of deep learning in remote sensing. Focus on the introduction and the sections on classification and segmentation.
2. Helber, P., Bischke, B., Dengel, A. and Borth, D. (2019) EuroSAT: a novel dataset and deep learning benchmark for land use and land cover classification. *IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and Remote Sensing* 12(7), 2217–2226. https://doi.org/10.1109/JSTARS.2019.2918242
   *Why:* the dataset used in this week's lab.
3. Rolf, E., Klemmer, K., Robinson, C. and Kerner, H. (2024) Position: Mission critical – satellite data is a distinct modality in machine learning. ICML 2024. **Open access:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01444
   *Why:* argues that satellite data need their own methods, not borrowed computer vision.
